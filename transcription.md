
# Demo Video Transcription: CGPA Analysis and Querying System

**Video Style:** Fast-paced and engaging at the start, then a clear and steady walkthrough. Upbeat, inspiring background music that quiets down during voiceover explanations.

---

### **Part 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:15)**

**(0:00 - 0:05)**
*   **Visual:** Fast-paced, cinematic shots of the application's UI. Quick cuts showing the login screen, the dashboard with charts, and the custom query page. Text overlays appear and disappear quickly: "Tired of manual calculations?", "Want to understand your grades?", "Ready for AI-powered insights?".
*   **Audio:** Upbeat, energetic, inspiring music. No voiceover yet.

**(0:05 - 0:15)**
*   **Visual:** The screen settles on the beautiful `Start` page of the application. The project's name "CGPA Analysis and Querying System" is clearly visible. A cursor dramatically clicks the "Get Started" button.
*   **Audio:** Music swells and then fades to a lower volume as the voiceover begins.
*   **Voiceover:** "Stop drowning in grade sheets and spreadsheets. What if you could understand your academic performance and get answers to complex questions about your grades, just by asking? Introducing the CGPA Analysis and Querying System, the smartest way to track and analyze your results."

---

### **Part 2: The Walkthrough (0:15 - 1:45)**

**(0:15 - 0:30)**
*   **Navigation:** The user is on the `Register` page.
*   **Visual:** Show the registration form being filled out. The user enters their ID, name, password, and selects a sample PDF grade sheet for upload. The process is slightly sped up.
*   **Audio:** Calm, clear voiceover.
*   **Voiceover:** "Getting started is simple. Create a secure account by providing your university ID and uploading your latest semester's grade sheet. Our system instantly parses the PDF to build your academic profile."

**(0:30 - 0:45)**
*   **Navigation:** The user is automatically redirected to the `Login` page after registration, and then logs in. They land on the `Overall Analysis` dashboard.
*   **Visual:** The main dashboard is displayed. Showcases the overall CGPA, total credits, and other key stats in `StatCard` components. The cursor hovers over each stat to draw attention.
*   **Voiceover:** "Once logged in, you're greeted with your personalized dashboard. Immediately, you can see your most important metrics: overall CGPA, total credits earned, and your current academic standing."

**(0:45 - 1:05)**
*   **Navigation:** Scroll down on the `Overall Analysis` page.
*   **Visual:** The screen focuses on the grade distribution chart. The chart is animated, with bars growing into place. The cursor hovers over different bars to show the count for each grade (S, A, B, etc.).
*   **Voiceover:** "Curious about your grade distribution? A clean, visual chart shows you exactly how many of each grade you've achieved, helping you see your strengths at a glance."

**(1:05 - 1:20)**
*   **Navigation:** Click on the `Semester Analysis` tab in the sidebar.
*   **Visual:** The view switches to the semester-wise breakdown. A table or list shows the SGPA for each semester. A line chart above the table visualizes the academic progression over time.
*   **Voiceover:** "Track your growth over time. The Semester Analysis page breaks down your performance, showing your SGPA for each semester. This helps you visualize your academic journey and identify trends."

**(1:20 - 1:45)**
*   **Navigation:** Click on the `Custom Queries` tab in the sidebar.
*   **Visual:** The screen shows the custom queries page with a text input field. The user types a natural language question like: "In which semester was my SGPA the lowest?" and hits enter. The answer appears below almost instantly. Then, the user asks another question, "How many S grades do I have in total?". The answer is displayed.
*   **Voiceover:** "And now for the most powerful feature. Have a specific question? Just ask. Our AI-powered query system understands natural language. You can ask complex questions like 'In which semester was my SGPA lowest?' or 'How many S grades do I have?' and get immediate, accurate answers. No more manual searching!"

---

### **Part 3: The Conclusion (1:45 - 2:00)**

**(1:45 - 2:00)**
*   **Visual:** Quick cuts of the most visually appealing parts of the app again: the animated chart, the dashboard, and the AI query in action. The video ends with the application logo or name and a call to action.
*   **Audio:** Music swells up again.
*   **Voiceover:** "From detailed analysis to AI-powered insights, the CGPA Analysis and Querying System is your personal academic advisor. Take control of your grades. Understand your journey. Try it now."
*   **On-screen text:** Project URL/GitHub link.
